About Patsav Academy

A training academy for people who want to work in music, sound, light, events and digital media.

Our approach

Patsav Academy brings the studio, the stage and the screen together in one place. You learn by doing: mixing a track, running a sound check, designing a light show or launching a website.

Our trainers work in the industry, so lessons follow what clients and audiences expect today. Batches stay small so you get time on the equipment and feedback on your work.

What you get

  • Practical training on professional equipment and software
  • A portfolio of projects you can show to clients and employers
  • Guidance on starting freelance work or joining an event team
  • Certificate on completion
